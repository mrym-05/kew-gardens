Must-See Attractions

From tropical rainforests to quiet Japanese gardens, discover six unforgettable places to begin your own Kew story.

Japanese Landscape

Discover a peaceful Japanese-inspired garden with winding pathways, carefully selected plants and stunning seasonal displays.

Walled garden

Explore a beautifully restored garden featuring colourful flower borders, historic charm and carefully maintained seasonal planting displays.

The palm house

Explore an iconic Victorian glasshouse filled with exotic tropical plants, towering palms and vibrant greenery from around the world.

Treetop walkway

Discover stunning views above the treetops while exploring a unique walkway surrounded by nature and woodland landscapes.

Princess conservatory

Explore ten unique climate zones showcasing rare plants, colourful orchids, unusual desert species and exotic greenery world-wide.

king william's temple

Discover a peaceful neoclassical temple framed by mature trees, lush planting and beautiful garden scenery throughout the seasons.

A little planning goes a long way. Check opening times and any attraction closures before you travel.
